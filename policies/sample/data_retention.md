# Data Retention Policy
Policy ID: DATA-RET
Domain: retention

## 1 Scope
This policy applies to all systems that store customer or employee records.

## 2 Retention periods
Systems that store customer information must define a retention period and enforce it with an automated deletion or lifecycle rule. (risk: high; evidence: retention)
Customer records must not be retained for longer than 7 years unless a legal hold applies. (risk: medium; evidence: retention)

## 3 Backups
Backups must follow the same retention period as the primary data store. (risk: medium; evidence: retention)
